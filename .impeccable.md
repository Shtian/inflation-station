## Design Context

### Users
Solo use — a single person doing periodic spot-checks on personal spending. The primary job is to quickly orient: how much did I spend, what categories, is anything unexpected? Secondary jobs are CSV imports and monthly AI reviews. The interface is a personal tool, not a product — it should feel like a well-maintained notebook, not a polished SaaS app.

### Brand Personality
**Sharp. Calm. Personal.**

Quiet confidence — no decoration for its own sake. Every element should feel chosen, not defaulted. The palette is warm-neutral (earthy orange accent against achromatic grays), evoking parchment and ink rather than dashboards and data rooms.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark mode default. Warm-neutral achromatic base with an earthy orange (`oklch ~0.65-0.72 C0.13-0.14 H48`) as the single accent hue. No other chromatic colors except semantic success (muted green) and warning (amber) states.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans for UI text — warmer and more distinctive than Inter/Geist, excellent at small sizes. Geist Mono for all numeric and code content, reinforcing data precision.
- **Reference aesthetic**: https://o16g.com/manifesto/ — appreciate the warm palette progression (gold to cool), the hand-crafted quality, generous whitespace, and intellectual directness. The manifesto's sense of "purposefully minimal" is the target feeling.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards (Stripe-clone grids, hero metrics with gradients, cyan-on-dark AI aesthetics). Nothing should look like it came from a template.
- **Motion**: Minimal and purposeful. No bounce easing. Loading states use simple opacity/text — not elaborate skeleton forests.
- **Layout**: Content-first. Avoid nesting cards inside cards. Generous separators over visual boxes where possible.

### Design Principles
1. **Every color earns its place.** The earthy orange accent appears sparingly — focus rings, active nav state, primary actions. Semantic colors (destructive, success, warning) are tokens, never raw Tailwind scales.
2. **Data density over decoration.** Tables, filters, and charts take priority. No hero sections, no padding theater.
3. **Warm neutrality.** The achromatic palette should read as warm, not cold. Slightly tinted rings, slightly warm card surfaces. The orange accent does the work of branding without shouting.
4. **Accessible by default.** Dark-mode errors must be visible. All interactive elements have adequate touch targets and focus rings. Status messages use `role="alert"` or `aria-live`.
5. **Personal, not productized.** Copy is direct and functional. No marketing language. No redundant section headers that restate what the page title already says.
