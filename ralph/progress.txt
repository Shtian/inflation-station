## Codebase Patterns
- Keep cross-route navigation in `src/app/layout.tsx` so route-level pages can be introduced incrementally without duplicating nav markup.
- During route-split migrations, keep `/` as a temporary legacy workspace and document transitional routing behavior in `README.md`.
- Keep overview analytics filter state and `/api/dashboard/analytics` fetching logic scoped to the `/overview` route UI instead of the legacy `/` workspace.
